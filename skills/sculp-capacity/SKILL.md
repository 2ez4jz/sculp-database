# SCULP Capacity
Count unique bookings once; distinguish primary artist attribution from collaborators and actual labor hours. Check location, travel, prep, duration and available staffing before alleging conflict or underutilization. Split inquiry versus confirmed versus completed bookings. Recommend a capacity action only when the diary and required times are available, with an observable follow-up measure.
