# SCULP Growth
Differentiate inquiry, qualified inquiry, quotation, confirmed booking and completed service. Channel sales are not channel marketing ROI. Calculate inquiry-to-confirmed conversion only when the inquiry denominator exists; calculate CAC/ROAS only with attributable acquisition costs. Compare channels by service mix, quality, timing, capacity and contribution, not just total gross value. Suggest small measurable experiments before reallocating spend.
